Auto parts billing software that makes the bill in seconds

AutoParts POS is billing software built for spare parts shops. Search by part number, name, brand or the car it fits, add the item to the bill, take payment and print. Customers do not wait while you look through a register.

Make a bill without mistakes

  • Search by part number, name, brand or vehicle, for example "brake pad corolla 2018".
  • Set discount %, tax % or a custom amount on any bill.
  • Preview the bill before you complete the sale, and print the same bill.
  • Pay in cash, card or bank transfer, and see the change due.

Receipts that fit your printer

  • Print on 80 mm receipt printers or on A4 paper.
  • Your shop name, logo and phone number appear on every bill.
  • Bill print users: each printed bill carries the name of the person who printed it.

Customer accounts and returns

  • Sell on credit and keep a running balance for each customer.
  • Print or view a customer statement showing every bill and payment.
  • Take returns: the items go back into stock and the customer balance updates.
  • Edit a saved bill if a price or quantity was wrong. Stock and the account are corrected.

Questions

Can I print receipts on a thermal printer?

Yes. Choose 80 mm receipt printer or A4 in the shop settings. Bills are laid out so the amounts fit inside the paper.

Can I sell on credit?

Yes. Add the customer, take a part payment if you like, and the rest stays on their account. You can print a statement at any time.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Billing works offline. The internet is only used to check your subscription and to sync data.
