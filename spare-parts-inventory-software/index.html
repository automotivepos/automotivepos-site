Spare parts inventory software that tells you what is on the shelf

Running out of a fast-moving part loses a sale. AutoParts POS keeps your stock up to date with every bill, shows what is running low, and lists what is already finished, so you know what to order before a customer asks.

Know what to reorder

  • Low stock and out of stock are shown as two separate lists. Tap either one to see only those parts.
  • Set a minimum quantity for each part. When stock falls to it, the part shows as low.
  • See the total value of your stock at cost.

Keep every part easy to find

  • Store the part number, name, category, bin location, cost and selling price.
  • Add the vehicles each part fits, so staff can search by car model.
  • Add stock when a delivery arrives, and see a history of every stock change.

Move your list in and out

  • Import your existing parts list from a CSV file (you can save one from Excel).
  • Export your stock to a CSV file at any time.
  • Back up your data, and sync it between your PC and your phone with a Google account.

Questions

Does stock go down automatically when I sell?

Yes. Every bill reduces stock, and returns put items back.

Can I sell parts by length or weight?

Yes. Each part has a unit, such as pieces or litres, and quantities can be decimals where the unit allows.

Can I import my existing parts list?

Yes. Use Import in the Inventory screen with a CSV file of your parts. Excel can save a sheet as CSV.
