Garage management software with job billing for workshops

A garage job mixes parts and labor, and the bill needs both. AutoParts POS lets you open a job for each vehicle, add parts from stock and labor charges as the work goes on, then turn the job into a finished bill.

One job for each vehicle

  • Record the number plate, car make and model, customer name and phone, and the work requested.
  • Add parts straight from stock. The list shows which vehicles each part fits.
  • Add parts you bought from another shop, with their cost.
  • Add labor lines, such as brake service, with their amount.

Finish and bill

  • Apply discount, tax or a custom amount to the job bill.
  • Take full or part payment. The balance goes on the customer account.
  • Save and close a job to continue later, then complete the bill when the work is done.

Works for the whole shop

  • Use it on a Windows PC at the counter and on an Android phone in the workshop.
  • Sync the same data on both with one Google account.

Questions

Can I keep a job open for several days?

Yes. Save and close the job, then open it again from the Garage jobs list.

Can I charge labor and parts together?

Yes. A job bill adds the parts and the labor lines, then applies discount and tax.

Can I add a part that is not in my stock?

Yes. Add a part bought from another shop with its quantity and cost.
