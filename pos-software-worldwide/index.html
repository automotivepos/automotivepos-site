POS software for auto parts shops, in any country

Auto parts shops work the same way in every country: many different parts, quick sales, regular customers and a lot of stock to track. AutoParts POS is built for that, and you set it up for your own country in a minute.

Set it up for your country

  • Type your own currency symbol, such as $, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR or Rs. Bills, statements and totals all use it.
  • Set the tax or VAT percentage on bills to match your local rules.
  • Print your shop name, address, phone and logo on every receipt.

Works for any shop

  • Search parts by number, name or the vehicle they fit.
  • Sell on credit and keep a printable statement for each customer.
  • Run garage job cards with parts and labor.
  • See low stock and out of stock lists to plan your next order.

Use it on any device

  • Windows PC, Android phone and web browser.
  • Print on 80 mm receipt printers or A4 paper.
  • Subscription prices are shown in US dollars.

Yes. Set a tax percentage on each bill. Check with your accountant for the rate your business needs.

The screens are in English today. You can type part names and customer names in any language.
